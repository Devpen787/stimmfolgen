# ADR 0004 - verify public availability separately from content cutoff

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: adopted for review preparation; release blocked.

The booklet's printed 29 November 2023 editorial cutoff cannot establish when the public could read it. A live historical archive acquisition found the exact official PDF at 11 January 2024, 12:11:17 UTC; its bytes, CDX digest and archived HTTP/Memento timestamps agree. The current publisher also dates the same-byte attachment to 11 January. Exact first publication remains unknown.

Keep this provenance in a sidecar keyed by the immutable document SHA-256. Preserve every historical report, span identity and model trace. Use an explicit proposed public pre-vote review frame of 2 March 2024, 23:59:59 CET, while retaining 29 November as the content cutoff. Reject public admission at a frame earlier than observed availability or on/after the Swiss voting day. Do not reinterpret an editorial cutoff, migration date, current retrieval date or publisher page-update date as publication.

Prepare review evidence, not review decisions. Bind a pending human review to source/catalog/reference hashes and immutable model candidates. Include original source-page context and disclose application-supplied types/refusal policy, composed model calls, coarse actor labels and reference-only entries. Human review, runtime temporal-frame adoption and final Gate 0 clearance remain outstanding. Frontend work stays blocked.
