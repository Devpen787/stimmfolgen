# Product assessment — 15 October 2026

This is the implementing agent's assessment, not judge feedback, independent user research or a predicted competition result.

## Customer value and present experience

The core journey is coherent: find a vote, track it without disclosing a choice, explore what it meant at a stated date, inspect sources, distinguish later official updates, consider another person's situation, and save a private reflection. The three primary navigation items stay simple. The responsive reader is usable, but the live area still carries substantial explanation/caveat text and can take time, fail or exhaust the shared allowance. These are meaningful service limits, not visual polish issues alone.

The differentiated mechanism is a source-bound historical reader with immutable quotations and attribution, explicit unknowns, separate later updates and a private encrypted notebook. It is not a new general voting assistant, a causal outcome evaluator or a complete vote registry. Five catalogue entries exist; only one AHV case has reviewed analysis, with five accepted statements. The native matcher remains experimental.

## Where Apertus actually helps

- Earlier native v1.5 inference extracted candidates from the authenticated federal booklet; deterministic span/identity/time checks and human decisions admitted the five published statements.
- The public live v1.5 8B endpoint interprets a visitor's question, translates it into an English source query, distinguishes observed later events and checks whether retrieved statements address the original question. It cannot write published factual fields or alter the accepted actor/quotation.
- Name routing and lexical ranking are application logic. Updates, relationships and perspective explanations are curated/reviewed content. Tracking, choices, note encryption and backups are browser features, not AI capabilities.
- A separate same-model relevance call is not independent semantic validation. Deterministic source/wording integrity and the recorded human approvals are separate checks.

Earlier native loopback installation proof exists on the owner's M3 Pro Mac; a complete fresh second-machine installation and broad local/hosted semantic parity are not established. Do not claim all deployment/submission requirements are cleared on the strength of the hosted demo alone.

The final 38/38 hosted questions are inspected development cases, not blind evaluation. The original Gate certificate remains PASS_BOUNDED_AHV, not general live accuracy. No second/unseen source import, physical-phone check, independent user study, load test, security audit, sustainable free service or production operations proof exists. The seven-day key, starter credit and shared caps limit availability. Existing report/video materials are not refreshed by this matching change; no video work was undertaken.
