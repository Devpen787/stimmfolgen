## Exact wording for your decision

These are statements about the 2025 proposal, not current personal tax advice. Open the official booklet linked above, pages 10 and 12. Accept all four, or name the statement needing a change:

1. **H01, page 10:** The linked reform would abolish imputed-rental-income taxation for owner-occupied homes and restrict deductions, including maintenance costs.
2. **H02, page 10:** Cantons could introduce a special tax on predominantly owner-occupied second homes. This would not happen automatically in every canton.
3. **H03, page 12:** Renters could also be affected by restrictions on interest deductions. The booklet says they would generally no longer deduct interest.
4. **H04, page 12:** The booklet does not establish one definite personal saving. Effects depend on residence, imputed rental value, deductions, taxable income, mortgage rates and any second-home tax.
