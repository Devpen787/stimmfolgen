About this demo

StimmFolgen helps you understand selected Swiss votes, check dated sources and keep your own reflections private. It does not recommend how to vote or calculate a personal tax bill.

How answers work

Apertus finds source information relevant to your question. The app displays source-bound wording and links rather than letting AI invent facts or quotations. AI can still misunderstand a question, so inspect the source before relying on an answer.

Privacy and availability

Tracked votes and notes stay in your browser. Saving after reload requires an encrypted vault and a passphrase you keep. There is no account or cross-device sync. Public questions sent to the online demo go through Cloudflare to Public AI. This is not exclusively Swiss hosting and its free allowance is limited. The optional on-premise version runs Apertus on your own Apple Silicon Mac.

For reviewers and judges

The original AHV source and human approvals have a bounded release certificate. This does not establish accuracy for arbitrary votes. The technical evidence includes model outputs, source hashes, failed experiments, the matching checks, installation instructions and known limits. Browser tests are agent-operated, not independent user studies.
