# StimmFolgen prototype walkthrough

Prepared locally on 14 October 2026. This is a narrated sequence of actual browser captures from the running reviewed-case app, not a continuous screen recording or fresh inference on a web click. No responses or source panels are fabricated. Voice is local macOS speech synthesis; no paid service or private user data is used. Captures, transcript and production script are reproducible. The MP4 is prepared for review; no external video-host upload or submission has been made.

## Narration

### overview
StimmFolgen helps a voter understand what a proposal could change, without recommending how to vote. This working prototype reads the original 2024 booklet about the thirteenth AHV old-age pension. The overview separates the proposed annual supplement, the government's cost projection, and the unresolved financing question. These are reviewed historical results from a real local Apertus run, not fresh generation when you click.

### recipient
Different situations raise different questions. For an old-age pension recipient, the proposed benefit is an annual supplement equal to one twelfth of the annual pension. This statement does not establish eligibility for every pension category. The source passage and the limits stay visible.

### personal-cost
For a working-age person, a definite additional personal charge cannot be established from the original initiative. When we check what is known, the application deliberately abstains from a CHF calculation. It explains that an enacted financing method and rate are missing. Unknown does not mean zero.

### attribution
Who said a prediction matters. This budget forecast belongs to the Federal Council and Parliament's argument section. The panel opens the verbatim source passage, page and hashes. Apertus independently extracted the source actor and parsed a requested actor; the application compared them without receiving the expected answer. Both sides' argument sections remain accessible.

### brief
The reader can write their own questions and export a brief. Notes stay in browser-tab memory, are never sent to the backend, and disappear on reload. The download is explicitly plaintext. This is not an encrypted vault.

### overview
Gate zero passed for this bounded AHV case: five human-approved statements and seven independently checked trials using eight real Apertus completions. Docker runs the reviewed publication journey locally. One referendum is verified; broader accuracy and a second case are still unproven. The goal is a clearer understanding of the proposal and its uncertainty.
